### The practice

Companies fall behind on AI for ordinary reasons — ownership gaps, sequencing mistakes, integration work that never became anyone’s job after the demo.

[Lapointe Labs](https://lapointelabs.com) is Marc Lapointe’s fixed-scope answer: a two-week audit that measures those reasons and sequences the fixes. The person on the call is the person in the repo.
